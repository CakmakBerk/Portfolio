My Story

Who Is Berk ÇAKMAK?

I'm Berk ÇAKMAK, a Computer Engineering student at TED University with a strong passion for software development, artificial intelligence, and user-centered design. Alongside my engineering education, I'm pursuing a secondary field in Business Administration and a Pearson BTEC Higher National Diploma, which helps me bridge the gap between technology and entrepreneurship.

My technical journey started in high school and was recognized with national success — including a 3rd prize at Teknofest 2019 and a 5th place in the First Lego League. Since then, I've developed various projects such as a Q&A-based Java game, an ERP appointment system, and LetFash, a mobile fashion platform powered by AI, Firebase, and Flutter.

I have advanced skills in Java, C#, and Dart, and hands-on experience with C, MySQL, MS SQL, and Flutter. During my internship at Agartha Teknoloji, I successfully built a Customer & Employee Management System using both Database-First and Code-First approaches in C#.

I'm currently focused on building innovative mobile solutions that blend AI, social media, and e-commerce — aiming to shape how users interact with fashion and virtual wardrobes.
