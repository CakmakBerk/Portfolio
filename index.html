My Story

Who Is Berk ÇAKMAK?

I'm Berk ÇAKMAK, a senior Computer Engineering student at TED University (GPA: 3.06/4.00). Alongside my engineering degree, I am concurrently pursuing a Secondary Field in Business Administration and a Pearson BTEC Higher National Diploma — building a foundation that connects software development with business thinking.

My interest in technology started early. In high school I and my friends earned 3rd place at Teknofest 2019 in the Technology for the Benefit of Humanity category, and 5th place with a Responsive Professionalism Award at the First Lego League. These early experiences shaped my drive to build things that matter.

On the professional side, I interned at Agartha Teknoloji, where I developed a Customer & Employee Management System in C# (.NET Framework) using both Database-First and Code-First approaches with MS SQL. More recently, I completed an internship at ASELSAN, where I developed black-box test automations using Python and Sikulix, executed YTET test procedures, and practiced defect lifecycle management within an Agile workflow.

I and my friends have also contributed to a research project accepted by Turkish Aerospace Industries (TAI) under the Lift Up Program, focused on vision-based navigation in GNSS-denied environments. Beyond work, I have been building LetFash — a Flutter/Firebase mobile app that blends AI, social media, and e-commerce for fashion.

İlter-Akke — Autonomous Drone Project

Project Details

A wearable hand gesture recognition system developed as a senior capstone project at TED University (CMPE 491/492). AKKE enables silent, secure, and real-time command-control communication in environments where speech is risky or impossible. Key areas include:

  • Flex sensor & IMU-based gesture recognition
  • Machine learning–based gesture classification
  • ESP-NOW wireless communication to team radios
  • FM audio transmission to standard military headsets
  • Embedded software on ESP32-S3 microcontroller
Technologies Used:

ESP32-S3, Python (ML Training & Classification), Arduino IDE, ESP-NOW, DFPlayer Mini, Flex Sensors, MPU6050 IMU, FM Transmission
